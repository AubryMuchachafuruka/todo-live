# Privacy Policy — Street League

**Developer:** MD.dev
**App:** Street League — Chitungwiza Street Champions
**Application ID:** com.muchafz.streetleague
**Effective date:** 2026-11-22

## 1. Local-first design

Street League is designed to work offline. Your player profile, experience points,
achievements, trophies, career history and season records are stored **locally on your
device**. No account is required to play offline, and we do not collect your name, email,
phone number or contacts.

## 2. What we store locally

- Your username (chosen by you) and avatar
- Player level, XP and XP transaction history
- Achievements unlocked and progress toward them
- Trophy room contents and competition history
- Season records and career milestones
- Challenge progress
- Local multiplayer match results (Bluetooth / WiFi)

This data stays on your device unless you choose to synchronise it.

## 3. Online features

Online multiplayer, friend lists, ranked matches and leaderboards require an internet
connection. Match results sent to the server are verified authoritatively before they affect
online rankings. Your username and match results may be stored on the server for the purpose
of leaderboards and anti-cheat verification. Server credentials are never embedded in the app.

## 4. Permissions

We request only permissions required by implemented features:

- `INTERNET` — online multiplayer and server verification
- `BLUETOOTH`, `BLUETOOTH_ADMIN`, `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`, `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` — local multiplayer
- `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`, `CHANGE_WIFI_STATE` — local WiFi / hotspot room discovery
- `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` — required by the operating system for Bluetooth device discovery
- `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (maxSdkVersion 28) — saving release builds and backups

We do not collect precise location data beyond what the OS requires for Bluetooth scanning,
and we never sell personal data.

## 5. Children

Street League is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance.
